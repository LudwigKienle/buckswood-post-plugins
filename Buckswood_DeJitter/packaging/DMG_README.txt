Buckswood DeJitter v1.0
=======================

Temporal micro-jitter removal for DaVinci Resolve.

Installation:

1. Quit DaVinci Resolve.
2. Open Buckswood_DeJitter_v1_Installer.pkg.
3. Complete the installer.
4. Restart Resolve.
5. Open Color > Effects > Buckswood > Buckswood DeJitter v1.0.

Start with View > Tracking Region and place Track Point on a fixed,
high-contrast detail. Avoid independently moving people, reflections, water,
screens, and strong motion blur.
