Buckswood DeJitter v1.1
=======================

Temporal micro-jitter removal for DaVinci Resolve.

Installation:

1. Quit DaVinci Resolve.
2. Open Buckswood_DeJitter_v1.1_Installer.pkg.
3. Complete the installer.
4. Restart Resolve.
5. Open Color > Effects > Buckswood > Buckswood DeJitter v1.1.

Start with View > Tracking Region and place Track Point on a fixed,
high-contrast detail. Drag the viewer handle, then use Fine X/Y for exact
placement. Optional Texture Snap moves the analysis center to a nearby rigid,
detailed feature.
