Buckswood DeJitter v1.2
=======================

Temporal micro-jitter removal for DaVinci Resolve.

Installation:

1. Quit DaVinci Resolve.
2. Open Buckswood_DeJitter_v1.2_Installer.pkg.
3. Complete the installer.
4. Restart Resolve.
5. Open Color > Effects > Buckswood > Buckswood DeJitter v1.2.

Enable Show Viewer Tracking Area. Drag the center cross or the inside of the
rectangle to place the Track Point on a fixed, high-contrast detail. Drag side
handles to change one dimension or corner handles to resize both dimensions.

The rectangle selects the analysis region. DeJitter stabilizes the full frame.
Use a Resolve node mask when the result should affect only part of the image.
