Buckswood Optics Lab v1.3.1
=========================

Independent optical and sensor finishing for DaVinci Resolve.

Installation:

1. Quit DaVinci Resolve.
2. Open Buckswood_Optics_Lab_v1.3.1_Installer.pkg.
3. Complete the installer and restart Resolve.
4. Open Color > Effects > Buckswood > Buckswood Optics Lab v1.3.1.

Glass, Dirt, and Smudge are selected directly inside the effect. The built-in
choices do not require a folder or separate asset install.

Optional licensed Glass assets:

Run Install_Licensed_Glass_Assets.command from this disk image. The helper
copies compatible aperture and dirt images from your own licensed Glass folder.
Paid assets are not included in this installer or public release.
